Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Glance Wallpaper. Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Android application. Glance Wallpaper is designed with a privacy-first approach — we do not collect any personally identifiable information from our users.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of the following:

  • Personal details (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Location data (GPS or approximate location)
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, Android ID)
  • Contacts, SMS, or call logs
  • Photos or files outside the app's scope
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Advertising identifiers

3. Information Stored Locally

Glance Wallpaper stores the following data only on your device:

  • Your favorite wallpapers (in a secure local database)
  • Your preferences and settings
  • Cached images for offline usage

This data is never transmitted to our servers or any third party. When you uninstall the app, all local data is permanently removed.

4. Third-Party Services

Glance Wallpaper uses the Unsplash API to provide high-quality wallpapers. When you search or browse wallpapers, anonymous API requests are sent to Unsplash's servers. We do not send any personal information with these requests. Photos are subject to the Unsplash License.

5. Permissions We Use

  • Internet: To fetch new wallpapers from Unsplash.
  • Set Wallpaper: To apply wallpapers to your home/lock screen.
  • Foreground Service: To change wallpapers automatically in the background.
  • Notifications: To show the ongoing wallpaper changer service.
  • Battery Optimization Exemption: To keep the auto-changer working reliably.

6. Data Security

All locally stored data is protected by Android's sandboxing system. No data leaves your device unless you explicitly share it.

7. Children's Privacy

Glance Wallpaper is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

  • Right to access: View all your data within the app.
  • Right to deletion: Delete all data via Settings → Clear Favorites.
  • Right to data portability: Your data is stored locally on your device.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app with an updated "Last Updated" date.

10. Advertising

Future versions of Glance Wallpaper may display advertisements to support ongoing development. When ads are introduced:

  • We may use Google AdMob as our advertising partner.
  • AdMob may collect your Advertising ID (AD_ID), approximate location, device information, and app usage data for ad personalization.
  • AdMob may share this data with its advertising partners in accordance with Google's Partner Sites Policy.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads at any time through your device settings:
    Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID
  • Non-personalized ads may still be shown.
  • When ads are introduced, we will update this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration accordingly.

For more information about how Google handles your data, please visit the Google Privacy Policy.
